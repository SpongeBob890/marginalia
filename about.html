About Marginalia

A small home for books and the people who write them.

Why we exist

Great books are easy to miss, especially when they come from independent authors without a big publisher behind them. Marginalia is a place to find them. We put books from independent and established authors side by side, so readers can stumble on something new and authors can be found by the people they wrote for.

The name comes from the notes people scribble in the margins of books they love. We like that idea: a book is worth more when someone shares what it meant to them.

What you’ll find here

Every book has its own listing with a cover, a description and a genre. You can search by title or author, filter by genre, and open the gift picks when you’re choosing a present. Author stories introduce the people behind the books, and more audio and video conversations are on the way.

How it works

When you find a book you like, “Buy on Amazon” takes you straight to its Amazon page, where you buy it as usual. You can like books, share them on WhatsApp, and see how many other readers have opened them.

For authors

Listing is free, and we review every book before it goes live. Your listing gives you a page for your book, reader numbers you can see, discovery by genre and easy sharing. Submit your book.

Say hello
