About Marginalia

A small home for books and the people who write them.

Why we exist

Great books are easy to miss, especially when they come from independent authors without a big publisher behind them. Marginalia is a place to find them. We put books from independent and established authors side by side, so readers can discover something new.

How it works

Every book has its own listing with a cover, description and genre. When you find one you like, “Buy on Amazon” takes you straight to its Amazon page, where you buy it as usual. Readers can like books and share them on WhatsApp, and authors can see how many readers opened their book.

For authors

Listing is free. Send us your book’s details and we review it before it goes live. Submit your book.
