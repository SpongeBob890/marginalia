Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for readers and authors.

Is Marginalia free to use?

Yes. Browsing, searching, liking and sharing books costs readers nothing, and there is no fee for authors to be listed. We never ask you to create an account or pay to look around.

Can I buy books directly on Marginalia?

No. Marginalia is a place to discover books, not a shop. When you tap “Buy on Amazon”, the book’s Amazon page opens in a new tab and you complete the purchase there, with Amazon’s usual checkout, prices and delivery to your device or Kindle app.

How do I list my book?

Go to the For authors page and fill in the form with your book’s title, your name, its genre, a short description and the link to your book on Amazon. Once you send it, we review the details before the book goes live, and we’ll email you if we need anything else.

Does it cost anything to be listed, and do you take a share of sales?

Listing is free, and we don’t take a cut. Readers buy from Amazon, so your sales, royalties and payments are handled entirely by Amazon and you keep your usual arrangement with them.

How long does a review take?

We review every submission by hand, so it isn’t instant. We check that the details are complete and the Amazon link works. If something needs fixing we’ll write to you, and once it’s approved your book goes live on the site.

What do the views and likes mean?

Views show how many times readers have opened a book from Marginalia. Likes show how many readers have hearted it. Your own likes are remembered on the device you use, so the heart stays filled when you come back.

What are gift picks?

They’re books we think make good presents, especially in the festive season. Tap “🎁 Gift picks” on the Books page to see them together, or find a selection on the home page. Each one has a red ribbon so you can spot it easily.

Can I share a book with a friend or family member?

Yes. Under every book there’s a “Share” link that opens WhatsApp with the book’s title, author and Amazon link already written for you. You only need to pick who to send it to.

Why can’t I find a particular book?

Marginalia only lists books submitted by authors or chosen by us, so it isn’t a complete catalogue. If you can’t find a title, try searching by author or genre. If you’re the author, you can submit it and we’ll take a look.

How can I contact you?

Use the contact form and your message goes straight to our inbox, or email patrickumoru595@gmail.com. We read every message, and we’re happy to hear questions, feedback and suggestions for books.
