Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for readers and authors.

Is Marginalia free?

Yes. Browsing is free for readers, and there is no fee for authors to list a book.

Can I buy books on Marginalia?

No. “Buy on Amazon” opens the book’s Amazon page, and the purchase happens there.

How do I list my book?

Fill in the form on the For authors page with your title, name, genre, a short description and your Amazon link. We review it before it goes live.

What do views and likes mean?

Views show how many times readers opened a book. Likes show how many readers hearted it.

Can I share a book with a friend?

Yes. Tap “Share” under any book to send it on WhatsApp.

What are gift picks?

Books we think make good presents this festive season. Tap “Gift picks” on the Books page to see them.
